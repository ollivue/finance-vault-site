Subscription Terms & Refund Policy

Application: Finance Vault · Effective Date: November 22, 2026 · Platform: Google Play Billing

1. Premium Plans & Pricing

Plan Billing Interval Price* Free Trial Renewal
Monthly Pro 1 Month ₹99 / $1.99 None Auto-renews monthly
Annual Pro 1 Year ₹699 / $14.99 14-Day Free Trial Auto-renews annually
Lifetime Pro One-time ₹1,499 / $29.99 N/A No renewal (Own forever)

*Prices include applicable taxes charged by Google Play. Prices are automatically converted to your local currency by Google Play upon checkout.

2. 14-Day Free Trial Terms (Annual Pro)

Eligible subscribers who choose the Annual Pro plan receive a 14-day free trial. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours prior to the trial expiration, your Google Play payment method will automatically be charged the annual plan price. You can cancel at any time during the trial at no charge.

3. How to Cancel Your Subscription

Uninstalling the app does not cancel your subscription. Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time via:

  • Direct Google Play Link: Manage or cancel anytime at Google Play Subscriptions (Finance Vault).
  • Inside the App (Active Subscribers): Open Finance Vault → Settings → License & Subscription → tap Manage or Cancel Subscription on Google Play.
  • In Google Play Store App: Tap your Profile Icon → Payments & Subscriptions → Subscriptions → Finance Vault → Cancel Subscription.

Upon cancellation, you will retain Pro access until your current billing period ends. Your local data is never deleted when a subscription ends.

4. Refund Policy

  • Within 48 Hours: You can request an automated refund directly through Google Play Order History.
  • After 48 Hours: Refunds are handled in accordance with Google Play policies. We do not provide partial pro-rated refunds for unused days within an active cycle.

5. Restoring Purchases

If you switch or reset devices, you can restore your active Pro subscription or Lifetime unlock with no extra charge: open Finance Vault → Settings → License & Subscription → tap Upgrade to Pro (or open any Pro feature lock prompt) → tap Re-activate Existing Pro while signed into the same Google Account.
