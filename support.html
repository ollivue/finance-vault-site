2. Frequently Asked Troubleshooting Questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?

Open Finance Vault → Settings → License & Subscription → tap Upgrade to Pro (or open any Pro feature lock prompt) → tap Re-activate Existing Pro at the bottom of the sheet. Ensure your phone is signed into the same Google Account used during the original checkout.

How do I move my financial data to a new phone?

Because Finance Vault does not store records in the cloud:

  1. On your old phone: Go to Settings → Backup & Sync → Migration → Export Portable Vault. Choose an encryption password and tap Export Vault File (.pfm).
  2. Transfer the .pfm file to your new phone (via USB, Nearby Share, or your preferred transfer method).
  3. On your new phone: Open Finance Vault → Settings → Backup & Sync → Migration → Restore from Vault File → tap Select & Restore File (.pfm) and enter your password.

What happens if I lose my backup encryption password?

Because your portable backup (.pfm) is encrypted on-device with AES-256 and zero passwords are sent to our servers, it is cryptographically impossible for us to recover or decrypt a lost backup password. Please store your password in a safe location.
