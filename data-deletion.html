Data Deletion Instructions

Application: Finance Vault · Official Public Deletion URL for Google Play
Zero Cloud Servers Notice

Because Finance Vault operates on an offline-first architecture, we do not store your financial entries on cloud servers. All accounts, receipts, and transactions are stored locally on your device. You have unilateral, instant control over permanently erasing your data.

Step-by-Step Data Deletion Methods

Method 1: Android System Storage Clear

To permanently delete all local financial records, preferences, and cached files while keeping the app installed:

  1. Open your Android device's Settings app.
  2. Tap Apps → Select Finance Vault.
  3. Tap Storage & cache → Tap Clear storage (or Clear data) and confirm.

Result: Android immediately and irrevocably purges the app's private sandboxed directory, including the local database (pfm.db), WAL journal files, encrypted entitlement vault, and attachments.

Method 2: Uninstall Application

To completely remove Finance Vault and all associated local data from your device:

  1. Open your device's Settings → Apps → Finance Vault (or long-press the app icon on your home screen).
  2. Tap Uninstall and confirm.

Result: Android automatically erases the application's entire sandboxed partition (/data/user/0/com.financevault.app/), leaving zero local records on your device.

Remote Data & Google Play Billing Records

  • Zero Server Retention: Finance Vault does not upload or store your financial data on any remote server, so there is no cloud account or server copy to erase.
  • Google Play Receipts: If you purchased Finance Vault Pro, Google LLC retains your billing transaction history in your Google Account in accordance with Google Play policies and statutory tax requirements.
