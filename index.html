Finance Vault is a modern, private personal finance manager built with double-entry arithmetic, nested categories, and sandboxed local storage with AES-256 encrypted backups. No bank links, no cloud servers, no tracking.

Get on Google Play

Zero Cloud Storage

Your transactions, balances, and budgets live exclusively in a private local SQLite database inside your device's app sandbox. We run zero cloud databases and cannot see your records.

Double-Entry Precision

True double-entry arithmetic ensures every unit of currency is accounted for. Transfers between accounts balance to zero and never distort your income or expense metrics.

Vector PDF Statements

Generate multi-page vector financial statements with crisp charts and detailed category breakdowns directly from your device without uploading data to external servers.

Hardware-Grade Security

Screen-capture prevention (FLAG_SECURE), private Android app sandboxing, and AES-256 encrypted portable vault backups (.pfm) you control with your own password.

Instant Offline Search

Full-text SQLite search engine indexing payees, descriptions, tags, and amounts with instant sub-millisecond query execution even with tens of thousands of records.

Multi-Currency Support

Comprehensive multi-currency ledger support, flexible decimal formatting, and customizable budgeting tailored for your everyday financial life.
