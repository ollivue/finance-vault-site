## 🔒 Privacy & Architecture

Finance Vault is engineered for strict personal financial privacy:

* **100% On-Device Storage**: All transactions, accounts, and budgets remain safely stored in a private, sandboxed local SQLite database on your device.
* **Zero Cloud Accounts**: No sign-ups, no passwords, no phone numbers, and no third-party data tracking.
* **Local Heuristic Analysis**: Cash flow trends, budget pacing, and category insights are calculated entirely on your device.
* **Encrypted Backups**: Portable backups (`.pfm`) and sync packages (`.pfmsync`) exported by the user are encrypted on-device with AES-256 / AES-256-GCM using your own password.
